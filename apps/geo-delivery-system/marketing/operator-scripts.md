# GEO Delivery System — 5 @theoperator scripts

Format: HOOK (0-2s) → TENSION (2-15s) → PROOF/DEMO (15-40s) → CTA.

## 1. The build — "I built the citation tracker agencies bill £2.5k/mo for, live"
HOOK: "Agencies are billing £2,500 a month for AI citation tracking. I'm building the tracker in n8n, on camera, in 40 minutes."
TENSION: "Dirty secret: most visibility tools run each prompt ONCE. Published testing says identical runs only overlap 32-43% on the sources they cite. One run is a coin flip wearing a dashboard."
PROOF/DEMO: Timelapse the n8n canvas: prompt battery → 5 runs per platform (ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity) → aggregation node → citation share WITH a range. Show the same prompt returning different citations run to run, then the range stabilising across runs.
CTA: "Importable workflow + the sellable offer around it — link in bio."

## 2. The honest one — the "error bars" methodology short
HOOK: "Every AI visibility score you've ever been shown is probably noise. Here's the 60-second test."
TENSION: Run the exact same prompt in ChatGPT five times, on screen. Different citations each time. "Research: 32-43% overlap between identical runs. Digiday: agency execs already call these scores 'a benchmark, not a source of truth'."
PROOF/DEMO: Side-by-side: a single-run scorecard ("You're #2!") vs the multi-run report ("cited in 6 of 10 runs, with a range"). "Which one survives the client doing their own five minutes of testing? The second. Every time. Error bars aren't weakness — they're the moat."
CTA: "The multi-run methodology is free on the site. The sellable system around it is £499 — bio."

## 3. The teardown — "Ranking #3, invisible in the AI answer"
HOOK: "This company ranks #3 for its money keyword. Watch me prove they're invisible where it now matters."
TENSION: "Top-10 rankings and AI Overview citations used to overlap ~76%. Early 2026: 17-38% (Ahrefs, BrightEdge). Ranking and being the answer have decoupled."
PROOF/DEMO: Live, names blurred: Google the keyword → AI Overview cites two competitors and a Reddit thread, not them → ChatGPT, same story. Fill in one page of the audit template on camera as the evidence lands.
CTA: "That one page is a £500-£2,000 audit engagement. The template's in the kit — bio."

## 4. The mistake — "SEO freelancers are defending the wrong number"
HOOK: "If your monthly report is explaining why clicks are down AGAIN, you're playing defence in a game that moved."
TENSION: "Seer: CTR down 61% where AI Overviews appear. But their own data shows it leveling off — so panic is a bad pitch, and informed clients smell it. The move isn't defending CTR. It's selling the new scope."
PROOF/DEMO: Split screen: a defensive SEO report vs the GEO proposal page — audit entry offer, £2-5k/mo retainer scope, methodology page with honest ranges. "Same client. One email defends the retainer. The other grows it."
CTA: "Proposal + pricing templates in the system — link in bio."

## 5. Build-in-public — "Week 1 of selling the pivot"
HOOK: "I launched a £499 system that turns dying SEO retainers into GEO retainers. Week 1, real numbers."
TENSION: Visitors, checkouts, refunds (if any), and the #1 objection so far — "is GEO measurement even reliable?" Fair question. That's why the tracker ships multi-run sampling with uncertainty ranges instead of pretending. Next objection.
PROOF/DEMO: Real dashboard on screen, warts included; first teardown posted in The GEO Room; the guarantee line on camera: 14-day refund if you can't send your first client proposal from it.
CTA: "System in bio. Numbers again next week, either way."
