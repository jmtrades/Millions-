Category: AI-service arbitrage | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec

Verdict: PASS (assessed 2026-07-10, hostile venture-gate pass).

- First-ever quarterly update (period 6 Apr–5 Jul 2026) is due 7 Aug 2026 — 28 days from today: https://www.freeagent.com/blog/mtd-for-income-tax-first-year-explained/ ; ~864,000 individuals drawn in for 2026/27 per https://www.step.org/industry-news/making-tax-digital-now-imminent-traders-and-landlords
- Next waves verified: >£30k from 6 Apr 2027; >£20k from 6 Apr 2028 (~970,000 additional): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-reducing-the-mandation-threshold-from-30000-to-20000-from-april-2028/reduction-of-the-mandation-threshold-from-30000-to-20000-from-april-2028
- Caveat that weakens the panic copy: HMRC will NOT issue penalty points for late quarterly updates in the first 12 months (late year-end filing still penalised) — https://www.step.org/industry-news/making-tax-digital-now-imminent-traders-and-landlords — so "miss 7 Aug and get fined" angles are factually wrong; capacity pain is real, sanction pressure in year 1 is soft.
Edge is concrete, correctly dated, in force 3 months, hard deadline 28 days ahead → timing gate passed.
