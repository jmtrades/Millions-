# Pay-Per-Meeting Outbound Engine
Category: AI-service arbitrage | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Done-for-you outbound: we build the ICP list, deep-research every prospect, write and send 1,000 tailored emails/mo from managed inboxes, and handle replies — the buyer pays only for qualified meetings that actually show on their calendar. Undercuts $3–14k/mo SDR agencies and removes their core failure mode (paying retainers for meetings that never convert). ~85% AI-automated (research agents, reply classification/handling), human review of edge-case replies.
- Buyer: Founder of B2B productized service or boutique agency (recruitment, dev shop, RevOps, design subscription), 1–15 staff, UK/US, ACV £2k–£30k, previously burned by an SDR agency or DIY-ing outbound.
- Price: £595 for a 5-qualified-meeting pack (£119/meeting), no retainer; inbox infrastructure included. Bought via Stripe from landing page; async onboarding (ICP form + calendar link).
- Channel to first 10: No dependence on the 692K/30K lists. Existing Skool community (agency owners are the exact ICP) + @theoperator audience; proof-first angle: every discovery call the founder takes was itself booked by the engine — "this meeting is the product." £0 spend.
- Timing hypothesis: (a) Buyer dissatisfaction is documented and quantified in 2026: outsourced SDRs run $3,000–$14,000/mo and a SaaStr survey found only 7% of companies say outsourced SDRs "really worked" (67% failed) — an incumbent priced for disruption; (b) per-prospect deep research + personalization cost collapsed to ~£0.01 via agentic web research with 50% batch-API and up-to-90% prompt-caching discounts (GPT-5.4 batch at $1.25/$7.50 per 1M tokens) — research-grade personalization at 1,000-prospect scale was not available as cheap API primitives in mid-2025.
