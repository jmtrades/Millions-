## R1 Spec
- Offer: The founder's actual one-person operating system, productized as a 40-asset vault + private Skool tier with lifetime updates. Transformation: an automation practitioner with real clients replaces improvised delivery with a proven OS. Inside: discovery→SOW pipeline, scoping calculator, delivery + handover checklists, agent monitoring/alerting stack (the dashboards that watch client automations overnight), retainer reporting automations, and the exact content engine that runs @theoperator.
