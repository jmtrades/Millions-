# OpsBrief (Reverse Marketplace for AI Automation Briefs)
Category: Marketplace | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Reverse marketplace: SMBs post budget-qualified AI automation briefs (problem, stack, £2K–£50K budget band, verified by a 10-min async intake); vetted AI agencies/freelancers pay to unlock briefs and respond (max 4 responders/brief). Market context: first AI implementations run $15K–$50K and 78% of orgs that deployed AI successfully used external partners — so one won brief repays years of unlock fees. Free side: SMBs posting briefs (free, they get 3–4 competing proposals).
- Buyer (paying side): AI automation agency owners and senior AI freelancers (1–15 person shops, the exact @theoperator follower profile) starving for qualified, budget-verified demand instead of content-marketing grind.
- Price: £49 per brief unlock (5-pack £199) or £249/mo unlimited in one vertical; founding cohort: £99 for first month unlimited to force week-1 cash.
- Channel to first 10: Announcement in founder's Skool community + @theoperator short-form video ("I built a board where qualified AI builds come to you") + direct DM to 50 agency-owner followers. £0 spend.
- Supply seeding: Demand (brief) side seeded without wait: (a) CTA email to the 692K cold list's business-owner segments offering free scoped AI-automation briefs with competing quotes, (b) founder converts inbound DMs/consult requests he already receives into the first 10–15 briefs, (c) aggregating public "who can build this automation" requests from r/automation, r/smallbusiness, and Facebook SMB groups (reposted with permission). Paying side is the founder's own audience — no cold start on the cash side.
- Timing hypothesis: AI-strategy consulting engagements grew 89% YoY into 2025 and SMB AI adoption stats show external-partner preference (41% of SMBs want a third party to run deployment), while agency supply has exploded and commoditized — 2025–26 is peak "demand can't tell good agencies apart, agencies can't find qualified buyers" fragmentation. Searchable: "AI consulting engagements grew 89% 2025", "SMB AI implementation partner".
