# UGC Brief Wire (Qualified Brand-Brief Feed for AI-UGC Creators & Micro-Studios)
Category: Marketplace | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Curated, qualified lead feed sold as subscription to AI-UGC video makers (solo creators and 1–5 person micro-studios using HeyGen/Creatify-class tools): 15+ verified brand briefs per week — DTC/app brands actively commissioning UGC-style ad creative — aggregated from scattered public sources, enriched with budget ($150–$500/video benchmark for human UGC; AI-UGC studios quote per-batch), format specs, and direct contact. One won brief (typical 5–10 video batch ≈ $750–$2,500) repays a year of the sub. Free side: brands, who post/get matched free (they're the scarce demand and must never hit a paywall).
- Buyer (paying side): AI-UGC creators and micro-studio owners monetizing avatar/UGC ad production (active on TikTok/X "AI UGC" scene; many are @theoperator viewers) who currently find briefs via chaotic Twitter/Discord scavenging.
- Price: £39/mo (or £299/yr); founding 50 at £24/mo.
- Channel to first 10: @theoperator short-form ("this week's 10 brands paying for AI UGC — how I found them") + X/TikTok repost, plus DMs to the visibly-selling AI-UGC creators quoting their own public "looking for clients" posts. £0 spend. Independent of founder email lists.
- Supply seeding: Aggregated day 1 via founder's scraping stack: brand briefs pulled from public UGC job boards, TikTok Creator Marketplace-adjacent postings, X/LinkedIn "looking for UGC creator" posts, and DTC brands' careers/creator pages — verified by checking the brand is live-spending (ad library check). No brand-side sale needed for revenue.
- Timing hypothesis: US creator-economy ad spend hits ~$37B in 2025 (+26% YoY) and 2026 testing shows AI-generated UGC matching human creator ads on CTR while beating them ~18% on CPA — a brand-new AI-UGC supplier class (tools at ~$39/mo displacing $150–$500/video creators) is forming faster than any brief-discovery infrastructure, leaving demand-discovery freshly fragmented. Searchable: "AI UGC ads CPA 2026", "creator ad spend $37 billion 2025".

## R2 Timing Gate
Verdict: **KILL-R2** (gated 2026-07-10, fresh web searches; standard: all three timing pillars must verify, wrong/vague kills).

1. **Creator ad spend ($37B 2025, +26% YoY) — VERIFIED.** IAB (Nov 2025): US creator-economy ad spend reaches $37B in 2025, +26% YoY, ~4x faster than total media (5.7%); $44B projected for 2026. https://www.iab.com/news/creator-economy-ad-spend-to-reach-37-billion-in-2025-growing-4x-faster-than-total-media-industry-according-to-iab/ · https://www.marketingdive.com/news/creator-ad-spend-to-reach-37b-in-2025-as-marketers-turn-to-ai-for-scale-i/805891/ — Only clean pillar, and it measures overall (mostly human) creator ad spend, not AI-UGC brief flow.
2. **AI-UGC performance ("matches human CTR, ~18% better CPA") — VAGUE/CONTESTED.** No source states 18%. Vendor-side: AI UGC reaches 85–110% of human-UGC CTR (https://pose.ai/blog/ai-ugc-vs-real-ugc-ads-2026); "up to 28% lower cost-per-result / 31% lower CPA" *in some campaigns*, and ~73% cheaper per asset ($150–500 human vs <$20 AI) (https://www.hoox.video/en/blog/ai-generated-ugc-vs-traditional-ugc-which-performs-better-in-ads). The largest cited independent-leaning test ($100K spend, inBeat agency) shows human CTR 2.4% vs AI 1.9% — AI ~21% WORSE on CTR (https://inbeat.agency/blog/ai-ugc-vs-real-ugc). The "~18% better CPA" figure is unattributable vendor-adjacent marketing; "matching CTR" is contradicted by the strongest available test.
3. **Supplier class "genuinely new (≤12mo)" — FALSE.** Dedicated AI-UGC ad tooling and the sell-AI-UGC scene date to 2024–2025, not post-mid-2025: Arcads described as "one of the earlier dedicated AI avatar video generators for UGC ads" (https://www.potionads.com/blog/best-ai-avatar-solutions-for-ugc-product-ads-2025); a "Top 8 AI UGC Video Generators for TikTok Ads in 2025" listicle ecosystem already existed in 2025 (https://usemintly.com/blog/top-8-ai-ugc-video-generators-for-tiktok-ads-in-2025); HeyGen and Creatify ship dedicated UGC-ad products (https://www.heygen.com/avatars/ugc · https://creatify.ai/use-cases/ugc); by 2026 mainstream monetization guides cover "AI-powered faceless" UGC (https://www.imagine.art/blogs/how-to-make-money-with-ugc). As of 2026-07-10 the class is ~18–30 months old.
4. **Bonus falsification of the "forming faster than any brief-discovery infrastructure" premise:** discovery infrastructure already exists at scale — 11+ ranked UGC job boards (https://www.pitchbrand.co/blog/the-top-11-ugc-job-boards-for-creators); JoinBrands ~250K creators / 20K brands, Collabstr 200K+ creators, Insense curated campaign briefs, Twirl structured briefs with "zero outreach needed" (https://fourthwall.com/blog/top-12-ugc-platforms-for-creators · https://hashmeta.com/blog/ugc-platforms-comparison-billo-vs-insense-vs-joinbrands-which-is-best-for-your-brand/); curated UGC-gig newsletters already exist, including free ones (Brands Meet Creators per PitchBrand's roundup; ugcjob.com's own newsletters — https://ugcjob.com/newsletters · https://newsletter.ugcjob.com/). "Demand-discovery freshly fragmented" is stale.
