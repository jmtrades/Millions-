Category: Marketplace | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec

- Buyer (paying side): UK acquisition entrepreneurs / self-funded searchers / micro-PE operators (ex-operators with £100K–£1M equity, active on X/LinkedIn ETA communities) hunting boomer-owned SMBs.

- Channel to first 10: Direct DM to the ~200 UK-visible ETA/searcher accounts on X and LinkedIn (searchable via "search fund UK", "buying a boring business"); post 2–3 public teardown threads of anonymized feed samples from @theoperator. £0 spend. Independent of founder email lists.
- Supply seeding: Fully scraped/aggregated day 1: Companies House bulk data + filed accounts + director DOBs is free public data; founder's scraping/enrichment stack builds the first 500-target feed in a weekend. No supplier consent needed to sell the research feed.
