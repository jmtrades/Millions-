# Succession Signals (Off-Market Retiring-Owner Deal Feed)
Category: Marketplace | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Curated off-market deal-flow feed sold as subscription to UK small-business acquirers: 50+ enriched targets/mo scraped from Companies House — trading companies 10+ years old, filed accounts implying £250K–£5M revenue, ALL directors aged 60+ (Companies House publishes director month/year of birth), no younger successor directors, no charges/insolvency flags — enriched with contact details and a one-line succession-risk score. Buyers currently pay brokers 5–10% success fees or trawl BizBuySell-style listed (i.e., already-competitive) deals; off-market origination is the scarce input. Free side: none at launch (pure aggregated-supply data feed — the sell side is public data, so zero chicken-egg); later, retiring owners can list free.
- Buyer (paying side): UK acquisition entrepreneurs / self-funded searchers / micro-PE operators (ex-operators with £100K–£1M equity, active on X/LinkedIn ETA communities) hunting boomer-owned SMBs.
- Price: £299/mo per sector+region lane (exclusive to 3 buyers/lane), or £999/qtr prepaid founding rate.
- Channel to first 10: Direct DM to the ~200 UK-visible ETA/searcher accounts on X and LinkedIn (searchable via "search fund UK", "buying a boring business"); post 2–3 public teardown threads of anonymized feed samples from @theoperator. £0 spend. Independent of founder email lists.
- Supply seeding: Fully scraped/aggregated day 1: Companies House bulk data + filed accounts + director DOBs is free public data; founder's scraping/enrichment stack builds the first 500-target feed in a weekend. No supplier consent needed to sell the research feed.
- Timing hypothesis: The "silver tsunami" has tipped into a buyer's market in 2025–26 — supply of retiring-owner businesses now outnumbers buyer demand, with ~2.3–3M boomer-owned SMBs transitioning this decade and only ~half having succession plans (Forbes, Jan 2026). UK-specific accelerant (hypothesis: Business Asset Disposal Relief CGT rate rises again in April 2026, pushing UK owners to accelerate exits — verify before build). Searchable: "silver tsunami buyer's market 2026".
- Citations: [https://www.clearlyacquired.com/blog/silver-tsunami-what-it-means-for-buyers, https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisoncoleman/2026/01/14/the-silver-tsunami-is-reshaping-small-business-why-exit-planning-cant-wait/, https://butcherjoseph.com/blog/business-sale/the-silver-tsunami-and-its-effect-on-the-ma-landscape, https://growamerica.org/2025/09/11/the-silver-tsunami-your-sba-lending-goldmine/]

## R2 Timing Gate
**Verdict: PASS (facts verified), with a material caveat that caps the timing score.** Reviewed 2026-07-10.

**BADR rate schedule — VERIFIED, exactly as hypothesized:**
- 10% on qualifying gains disposed of on or before 5 Apr 2025; **14% from 6 Apr 2025 to 5 Apr 2026; 18% from 6 Apr 2026** (vs 24% main CGT rate). Source: HMRC internal manual CG64174 (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64174); https://www.gov.uk/business-asset-disposal-relief; https://www.thewowcompany.com/blog/badr-changes; https://www.bdo.co.uk/en-gb/insights/tax/private-client/business-asset-disposal-relief
- Anti-forestalling rules apply to unconditional contracts entered from 30 Oct 2024 completing after the rate-change dates (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg64174).

**Acceleration effect — VERIFIED as a documented driver of UK exits:**
- UK advisers explicitly pushed owners to complete before 6 Apr 2026: "Clock is ticking for business owners planning an exit" (https://www.azets.com/en-uk/insights/clock-is-ticking-for-business-owners-planning-an-exit); SJP quantified Nov 2025 vs Jun 2026 sale at ~£40k more retained per shareholder per £1m gain (https://www.sjp.co.uk/individuals/news/timing-your-business-sale-november-2025-vs-june-2026-whats-the-financial-difference); fear of further tightening/abolition in future Budgets also documented (https://www.forthcapital.com/articles/if-entrepreneurs-relief-is-scrapped-in-the-autumn-2025-budget-what-will-this-mean-for-uk-business-owners; https://brodies.com/insights/wills-and-estate-planning/business-asset-disposal-relief-badr-what-is-changing-on-6-april-2026/).

**Silver-tsunami buyer's market — VERIFIED but largely US-sourced:**
- "Supply is rising faster than demand... a true buyer's market" (https://smb.co/blog/silver-tsunami-millions-of-small-businesses-need-new-owners; https://www.clearlyacquired.com/blog/silver-tsunami-what-it-means-for-buyers); ~2.3–3M US boomer-owned SMBs transitioning this decade, ~70% without succession plans (https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisoncoleman/2026/01/14/the-silver-tsunami-is-reshaping-small-business-why-exit-planning-cant-wait/; https://www.bizbuysell.com/blog/silver-tsunami-shift-gen-x-leads-small-business-market/). UK-specific corroboration: BizCrunch's own research claims 100,000+ UK companies with >£1m revenue coming up for sale as owners hit retirement age (https://www.bizcrunch.co/blog/roll-ups-are-the-new-startups).

**Hostile caveat (why timing scores only 5):** as of today (10 Jul 2026) the 18% rate has been IN FORCE for 3 months. The candidate's stated accelerant — "BADR rate rise Apr 2026 pushing owners to accelerate exits" — was true but is now SPENT: the beat-the-deadline rush completed before 6 Apr 2026, and documented pull-forward implies a plausible H2-2026 sell-side lull. What remains is the structural demographic wave plus fear of further Budget tightening, not a dated catalyst. Not wrong, not vague → no kill, but the sharpest timing edge has expired.
