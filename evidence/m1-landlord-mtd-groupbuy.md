# Landlord MTD Club (Group-Buy of MTD Software + Filing Packages)
Category: Marketplace | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Aggregated-demand buying club: UK landlords/sole traders hit by Making Tax Digital pay a small annual membership to access founder-negotiated group rates — target 30%+ off MTD-compatible software annual plans and fixed-fee quarterly-filing packages from partner bookkeepers (vs the 5–10% accountant price INCREASES the market is imposing). Membership also includes the deadline-calendar tool and a members-only Q&A. Unit economics for the member: software + filing typically £300–£800/yr; a 30% saving (£90–£240) dwarfs the £49 fee. Free side: software vendors/bookkeepers, who give discounts in exchange for aggregated committed volume (they pay nothing; they gain hundreds of customers in one deal).
- Buyer (paying side): UK private landlords and sole traders with >£50k qualifying income who are inside MTD from April 2026 and DIY-minded (won't pay a full-service accountant). Distinct buyer type from the accountant-side play.
- Price: £49/yr membership, charged upfront via Stripe; founding cohort £29/yr for the first 200 to force velocity before the 7 Aug deadline.
- Channel to first 10: Post the free "MTD cost calculator + group-rate waitlist" into Property118 and LandlordZone forums, r/uklandlords, and the large UK landlord Facebook groups where MTD panic threads are running now; plus the UK landlord/sole-trader segment of the 692K list. £0 spend.
- Supply seeding: No chicken-egg: founder pre-negotiates ONE anchor deal before launch (MTD software vendors run partner/affiliate programs as standard, so a 20–30% group rate against committed volume is a 2-call BD task); memberships are sold against the anchor deal, and additional vendor deals are added with member volume as leverage. If a formal exclusive stalls, launch on published partner-program rates + negotiated bookkeeper fixed fees (founder-curated).
- Timing hypothesis: MTD for Income Tax became mandatory 6 April 2026 for ~780,000 sole traders and landlords; MoneyWeek reports 200,000+ face accountant cost hikes of up to 10% as demand surges, and the first quarterly deadline of 7 August 2026 lands in 4 weeks — cost-panic + hard deadline is the exact condition group-buying converts on. Searchable: "Making Tax Digital accountant costs rise 2026".

## R3 Saturation Gate
VERDICT: KILL-R3 — saturation 7/10 (crowded/established). The "pay an annual fee → get landlord discounts + tools + deadline support" slot is already owned at scale by the NRLA: £125/yr individual membership, 50+ supplier discount partnerships, free NRLA Portfolio property-management software for members, and MTD filing bundled via the March 2026 Taxd partnership (https://www.nrla.org.uk/join ; https://www.nrla.org.uk/member-benefits ; https://rentalbux.com/compare/rentalbux-vs-nrla). Beneath it sit other associations with member perks (https://www.totallandlordinsurance.co.uk/knowledge-centre/benefits-landlord-association) and a floor of free/near-free substitutes that vaporise the 30%-off math: 9+ free HMRC-recognised MTD products (https://figsflow.com/8-free-hmrc-approved-mtd-software-for-accountants-and-tax-agents/), Landlord Studio free ≤3 properties and FreeAgent free via NatWest/RBS/Mettle (https://anna.money/blog/guides/making-tax-digital-software-for-landlords/), VitalTax bridging ~£30/yr (https://mtd.digital/mtd-income-tax/mtd-bridging-software/), and HMRC's own free tool (https://makingtaxdigital.campaign.gov.uk/making-tax-digital-software/). A £49/yr club selling 30% off products credible competitors give away for £0 — with zero deals actually negotiated yet (spec admits the anchor deal is a plan, not a fact) — enters a category the dominant association already gives its members as table stakes.

## Proposed Scores
- pain: 4 — the club addresses software/filing COST, not compliance itself; with free HMRC-recognised software and a ~£30/yr bridging floor (URLs above), the cost pain is small even though accountant fees are rising (moneyweek.com above).
- reach0: 3 — channel includes the 692K list segment (liability cap ≤3); forums unowned and moderated.
- ttfd: 2 — first honest paid transaction requires vendor group-rate deals that do not yet exist; negotiating leverage requires volume the club doesn't have (chicken-egg the spec hand-waves); >60 days, and charging members before deals exist is a consumer-protection trap for this audience.
- margin: 9 — membership revenue, ~zero COGS.
- timing: 9 — hard dated deadline cluster-verified (7 Aug 2026; Apr 2027 wave).
- fit: 3 — vendor BD/negotiation is a new skill with no tech leverage; no landlord distribution asset; Stripe checkout is the only stack overlap.
