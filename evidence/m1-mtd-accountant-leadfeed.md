# MTD Lead Desk
Category: Marketplace | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Qualified-lead feed sold as subscription. UK landlords/sole traders (>£50k qualifying income, now legally inside MTD for Income Tax) submit a brief via a free "MTD penalty & cost checker" tool; accountants/bookkeepers get 10–25 verified, region-filtered leads/mo (name, income band, software status, deadline urgency). One converted client is worth £1,000–£2,500/yr in fees (accountants raising MTD prices 5–10%, 47% plan increases), so one close pays for 6–12 months of the sub. Free side: landlords/sole traders (pay nothing, get matched quotes).
- Buyer (paying side): Owner/partner of UK accounting or bookkeeping practice, 1–25 staff, serving sole traders/landlords, actively marketing MTD services. Free side: UK landlords and sole traders above the £50k threshold.
- Price: £199/mo per region subscription (cap 5 firms/region); £89 one-off "founding member" first month to collect cash in week 1.
- Channel to first 10: Cold email 2,000 UK practices scraped from ICAEW/ACCA public member directories + firms already publishing MTD blog content (self-identified demand) using founder's own sending infrastructure; parallel posts in AccountingWEB forum and UK Accountants Facebook groups. £0 spend.
- Supply seeding: Demand side seeded without waiting: (a) free MTD penalty checker lead magnet promoted in landlord forums (Property118, LandlordZone, r/uklandlords), (b) UK sole-trader segment of the 692K cold list, (c) aggregating public "need an accountant for MTD" posts from Reddit/forums into the first week's feed. No two-sided wait: sell subs on a committed lead-volume SLA with refund if under-delivered.
- Timing hypothesis: MTD for Income Tax went live 6 April 2026; the FIRST quarterly submission deadline is 7 August 2026 — 28 days from today — for ~780,000 sole traders and landlords, with penalty points thereafter. Accountant capacity is the bottleneck (150 clients → ~750 filing events/yr), so firms are actively buying growth while taxpayers scramble. Searchable: "MTD ITSA 7 August 2026 deadline".
