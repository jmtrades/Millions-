# EAA Accessibility Compliance Pack for EU-Selling Stores

Category: Productized service | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: 7-day EAA Compliance Pack: WCAG 2.1 AA automated scan of the top-10 templates + manual keyboard/screen-reader pass on the money paths (PDP -> cart -> checkout), the legally required accessibility statement drafted for their store, a prioritized remediation list with code-level guidance, and the top 10 theme-level fixes implemented. Evidence folder formatted for a market-surveillance inquiry.
- Buyer: Owners / heads of e-commerce at DTC Shopify brands (£1M–£20M GMV) based in the UK/US that ship to EU consumers.
- Price: £1,200 one-off (accessibility firms' manual audits alone commonly run far higher; low-end fixed audits exist at ~€319 with no statement or fixes — this bundles audit + statement + fixes).
- Channel to first 10: white-label partnership offer to 3–5 Shopify agencies (they resell at margin) + answer-posts in r/shopify and Shopify Community threads asking "does the EAA apply to my store?". Independent of the 692K/30K lists.
- Timing hypothesis: the European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025; the first EAA lawsuits were filed in France in Nov 2025, the Dutch ACM is actively enforcing against e-commerce, and 2026 is the first full year of member-state supervision with fines ranging ~€60k (Ireland) to ~€900k (Sweden) — deadline passed, enforcement starting, most non-EU DTC brands still unaware they're in scope. Scanning + statement drafting ~85% automatable (axe-core + templated statement + AI-drafted fixes); <4 founder-hours/unit.

## R4 Red Team
- Strongest failure case: every EAA court action to date targets giants — Carrefour (4 Jun 2026 order) and Auchan, where the retailer WON because the Lille court applied a €250M domestic threshold (https://silktide.com/blog/eaa-auchan-court-ruling/); as of Jul 2026 there is no documented fine or court order against any SMB or any non-EU store, and NL "active enforcement" is still prospective (expected H2 2026 — https://www.levelaccess.com/blog/eaa-compliance-in-2026-how-enforcement-has-evolved-and-what-to-expect-next/). Urgency for a £5M UK/US Shopify brand is therefore anticipatory, sold on fear — and a fear-driven £1,200 purchase collapses if the buyer googles for five minutes and finds only Carrefour. Secondary exposure: a solo non-lawyer selling compliance assurance — the FTC fined accessiBe $1M (final order Apr 2025) precisely for overclaiming that its product delivered WCAG compliance (https://testparty.ai/blog/accessibe-alternatives); an "evidence folder for market-surveillance inquiry" pitched too hard invites the same overclaim/negligent-misstatement liability if a client gets fined anyway.
- Strongest rebuttal: the Dutch ACM has concrete, dated machinery that reaches this exact buyer — worldwide information requests already sent (including to companies with no EU office), a statutory duty for non-compliant shops (>10 staff or >€2M turnover) to SELF-REPORT with a remediation plan, checks of online stores continuing per the 2026 ACM agenda, and fines to €900k or 1–10% of turnover (https://www.acm.nl/en/accessibility/accessibility-e-commerce-services-and-electronic-communications-services , https://www.acm.nl/en/publications/acm-calls-businesses-prepare-themselves-new-rules-regarding-accessibility-websites-and-apps , https://www.acm.nl/en/publications/focus-areas-acms-oversight-over-digital-economy-2026). The deliverable (audit + statement + plan + evidence folder) maps 1:1 to what ACM asks for, Caen's "obligation of result" standard kills the "we tried" defence, and £1,200 with fixes included sits below audit-only market pricing. Liability is mitigable by scoping language: sell "audit + fixes + statement drafting", never "compliance certification/guarantee" — the exact line accessiBe crossed.
- Defensible: TRUE (narrowly). The ACM self-report duty + worldwide info requests are searchable facts that make the offer rational for the target buyer today, independent of whether a giant-only lawsuit wave ever reaches SMBs.
