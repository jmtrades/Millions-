Category: Productized service | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: 5-day AI Findability Audit: how the team surfaces across ChatGPT (incl. the new Realtor.com ChatGPT app), Zillow/Redfin AI surfaces, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode for a 25-prompt battery of local buyer/seller queries in their farm area; side-by-side citation comparison vs 3 named competitor teams; fix pack covering agent profiles, schema markup, GBP, and review entities. PDF report + prioritized 30-day action list.
- Buyer: Residential real-estate team leads and broker-owners (teams of 2–20 agents) in US metro markets (30K real-estate agent email list — geography assumed US).
- Price: $399 one-off (≈£299).
- Channel to first 10: the 30K real-estate agent email list, sent from founder's own compliant cold-email infra. Angle: "Buyers now ask ChatGPT for agents. Real estate ranks dead last of all industries in AI search visibility — here's exactly where your team shows up (and doesn't)." Free 3-prompt teaser screenshot per reply.

Prompt-battery runs are ~95% automatable; <2 founder-hours/unit.
