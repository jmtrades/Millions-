## R4 Red Team (failure case: DEFENSIBLE) — verified 2026-07-10
Strongest failure case: liability-credibility mismatch. The kit is employment-law-adjacent content from a founder with zero HR/legal standing, sold to buyers who are professionally liable for what they resell. Searchable facts: (1) a whole PI-insurance product category exists for HR consultants because incorrect employment-law advice generates negligence claims — tribunal-driven awards "in the tens of thousands", discrimination awards uncapped, and aggregation risk where one flawed policy touches every employee of every client; recommended indemnity £1m-£3m for a small consultancy (https://apexinsurancebrokers.co.uk/wiki/hr-consultants-pi-insurance-uk/; https://www.hiscox.co.uk/business-insurance/hr-consultants; https://www.policybee.co.uk/hr-consultants-insurance; https://www.markeluk.com/business-insurance/hr-consultant-insurance). A consultant reselling white-label ERA collateral bears that exposure — provenance from an anonymous automation creator with no legal review is a hard objection, on content whose official timeline has already shifted once (3 Feb 2026 revision). (2) The buyer population is CIPD-professionalised and already receives authority-backed ERA resources with membership plus a purchasable CIPD HR-inform ERA Toolkit (https://www.hr-inform.co.uk/employment-rights-act). (3) The named £0 channels gate vendor access: HR Ninjas monetises vendors via paid partnership; HRi is a paid membership body.
Strongest rebuttal: the kit's legal substance is fully sourceable from citable free authority (gov.uk roadmap, Acas, law-firm alerts); the product is curation + client-facing production (deck, comms sequences, pricing calculator) that the consultant — the actual professional layer — reviews and stands behind, exactly as consultants already do with hrdocbox reseller-licensed templates from a non-law-firm vendor. hrdocbox's existence proves consultants do buy resale collateral from unglamorous vendors; the founder's 48h production quality and update automation are genuine differentiators.
Adjudication: rebuttal is real but partial — hrdocbox has HR-domain positioning and a track record; this founder has neither, and must pass trust screens in gated HR communities while selling liability-sensitive content. Failure case is DEFENSIBLE (true). Combined with fit ≤4 unrebutted and reach0 weakness, this kills the candidate for THIS founder despite verified timing.
