# VSME Supplier ESG Response Sprint (value-chain-cap era)
Category: R5 deadline-radar | Status: R5-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: 10-working-day done-for-you sprint producing a VSME Basic Module report (~50 datapoints, EFRAG digital template) plus a reusable answer matrix mapped to the big questionnaire platforms (EcoVadis, IntegrityNext, SupplierAssurance) — "answer once, reuse for every customer" — with a cover letter invoking the legal value-chain cap so oversized questionnaires can be lawfully pushed back.
- Buyer: Finance/ops directors at EU & UK SME manufacturers/logistics suppliers (20-250 staff) inside CSRD-scope customers' supply chains, who are losing tender points or facing procurement deadlines over unanswered ESG questionnaires; secondary: their accountants (resale).
- Price: £1,150 fixed (EUR 1,350). Stripe.
- Channel to first 10: targeted, compliant small-batch outbound (founder deliverability craft, fresh lists — never the 692K) to suppliers named on CSRD reporters' supplier pages / "EcoVadis requested" LinkedIn posts + @theoperator EU automation practitioners who serve manufacturers (resale) + Chambers of Commerce SME sustainability groups. £0 spend.
- Timing (verified): 2026 Omnibus I Directive makes the EFRAG VSME the statutory "value-chain cap" — CSRD reporters are legally barred from demanding more than VSME-scope data from partners <1,000 employees; wave-1 CSRD reports published spring 2026 have triggered a live questionnaire surge with rolling H2 2026 procurement deadlines. Teeth = lost contracts, not fines.
- Pass-pattern followed: (3) fixed-scope, low-liability deliverable priced in the £399-£1,200 band where incumbents (sustainability consultancies/platform subscriptions) are enterprise-priced or SaaS-self-serve; free EFRAG standard exists but is confusing/scattered for a no-ESG-capability SME.
- Kill-checks run: EFRAG standard + template free but DIY-hard; SaaS tools (QuickVSME, Sunhat) are self-serve platforms, not done-for-you — named distinctly to avoid collision; EcoVadis sells ratings, not VSME authoring; not a SOC2-procurement motion (buyer needs a document to answer a customer, fast).

## R4 Red Team (gate run 2026-07-10) — defensible: TRUE (conditional)
- STRONGEST FAILURE CASE (searchable facts):
  1. Data collection is the bottleneck the sprint cannot do. VSME preparation is benchmarked at 1-2 months "depending on the scope of the organization and ease of collecting data" (https://www.you-sustain.com/VSME). Energy bills, HR/headcount, waste and H&S data live with the client; the 10-day clock is hostage to client labor the £1,150 fee cannot buy.
  2. Wrong numbers = contract liability. Supplier ESG answers flow into customers' public CSRD reports and are increasingly locked into contractual ESG representations & warranties with audit rights (https://www.daeryunlaw.com/us/practices/detail/esg-compliance-review); "greenwashing lawsuits increasingly start inside bad data systems" (https://www.teilfirms.com/blog/greenwashing-litigation-is-no-longer-about-marketingit-starts-in-the-data-layer); EU/UK authorities gaining powers to fine misleading claims up to 10% of global revenue (https://www.optisolbusiness.com/insight/the-compliance-risks-of-missing-supplier-esg-data). A solo engineer with no ESG credential or PI insurance authoring numbers the client hands its biggest customer is concentrated tail risk.
  3. The cap cover letter markets "lawful pushback" citing a cap that is not yet operative law (delegated act in scrutiny until ~Q4 2026; directive transposition pending — see R2). Advising an SME to refuse its largest customer's questionnaire on premature legal grounds could cost the client the contract and edges into unauthorized legal advice.
- STRONGEST REBUTTAL: (i) gate the clock — "10 working days from data-room complete", with an automated evidence-chase checklist (founder's core automation skill) doing the collection choreography; (ii) VSME Basic is mechanical disclosure — founder compiles, client attests all inputs, liability-capped T&Cs (standard consultancy shield); (iii) reframe the letter as negotiating leverage citing the Commission's adopted 3-Jul-2026 text ("incoming law"), which matches already-published market practice for using VSME to manage value-chain requests (https://ga-institute.com/Sustainability-Update/managing-value-chain-data-requests-with-the-vsme/); (iv) answer-matrix reuse value is durable regardless of cap timing — EcoVadis alone has 150k+ rated companies (https://ecovadis.com/).
- VERDICT: defensible TRUE only with (i) data-room-gated clock, (ii) client attestation + capped liability, (iii) cap letter softened to "adopted, incoming" leverage. As literally spec'd ("legally barred", flat 10 days), FALSE.
