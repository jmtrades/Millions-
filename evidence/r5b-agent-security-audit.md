## R4 Red Team
Strongest failure case: an unaccredited solo engineer PASS-stamping client stacks is a liability magnet whose artifact has zero standing where security sign-off is actually enforced. (1) Auditor negligence liability runs in both contract and tort, extending to third parties who rely on the report — the agency's end client, exactly who the white-label PASS report is handed to: https://www.accaglobal.com/us/en/student/exam-support-resources/professional-exams-study-resources/p7/technical-articles/auditor-liability.html ; the "is the security auditor liable after a breach?" question is a long-standing live dispute: https://www.techdirt.com/articles/20090601/2312045091.shtml . White-labeling makes the founder the hidden deep-pocket counterparty behind someone else's brand while building zero brand equity. (2) Where sign-off is enforced (procurement), teams converge on CSA AICM/AI-CAIQ (243 controls) — a £499 PDF from an unaccredited individual satisfies none of it: https://www.atlassystems.com/blog/ai-vendor-risk-questionnaire . (3) The 25-point checklist is a weekend copy for any consultant: the OWASP framework is free and public (https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/) and a consultancy already publishes an n8n security-audit framework openly (https://www.wednesday.is/writing-articles/n8n-security-audit-and-compliance-framework). (4) Solo throughput: 5-day fixed-scope audits cap at ~4-6/month alongside content output → ~£2-3k/mo ceiling without hiring or productizing.
Strongest rebuttal: the buyer is the small agency facing a nervous SMB client — a tier BELOW where AI-CAIQ applies and where no accreditation for agentic stacks exists at all (the framework is ~7 months old; no cert body covers it) — so in that trust vacuum, first-mover audience trust IS the accreditation, and the founder owns the exact-ICP channel (@theoperator + Skool) that any copying consultant lacks. Liability is manageable with standard auditor practice: point-in-time scope, liability-capped engagement letters (ACCA-standard mitigations, same URL), PI insurance, and PASS/FIX framed as prioritized findings, not warranty. Throughput cap bounds downside during validation; fix-upsell raises revenue per stack.
Defensible: FALSE — checklist copyable, no accreditation moat, no artifact standing outside the founder's own audience; defensibility = distribution + speed only.
