## R4 Red Team
Gated 2026-07-10. VERDICT: defensible = true (narrowly).
- Strongest failure case: THE TRUST/PROCUREMENT WALL PLUS A POOR TARGET LIST. (1) SRA confidentiality guidance + Code of Conduct para 6.3: granting an unregulated third party access to client files/inbox/CMS is disclosure of confidential client information — firms must consider whether standard-terms consent suffices or specific informed client consent is needed, must run due-diligence on the provider, and remain fully accountable to the SRA for work carried out through others (https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/confidentiality-client-information/; https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/innovate/common-queries-answered/; https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/guidance/outsourcing-for-law-firms-compliance-lifecycle). A solo unregulated contractor asking for CMS + inbox access mid-rush is a COLP-level decision, not a £2,400 impulse buy — this stretches the 14-day-deposit thesis. (2) A large slice of the named IAA Level 2/3 channel is legal-aid/not-for-profit and financially hollow: immigration legal aid providers operate at "unsustainably low cost levels"; the 2025 30% fee uplift — first in ~30 years — is expected to be fully absorbed by staff retention, leaving no discretionary £2,400 (https://www.lawgazette.co.uk/news/legal-aid-fee-uplifts-not-high-enough-to-rebuild-sector/5123782.article; https://justice-together.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/No-Access-to-Justice-Report-2025.pdf). (3) Carried from R2: transitional arrangements could exempt existing residents and deflate the rush (https://www.ein.org.uk/news/ilr-earned-settlement-changes-proceed-says-immigration-minister-transitional-arrangements).
- Strongest rebuttal: Filter the list to PRIVATE-FEE immigration practices — the rush is private-client revenue (ILR/settlement fees per case exceed the install price; every extra case processed is direct fee income), and private firms hold the money the legal-aid segment lacks. SRA outsourcing guidance is a manageable checklist, not a prohibition — cloud CMS vendors, IT consultants and outsourced cashiers clear it routinely via confidentiality agreements + DPAs, which the founder can pre-package (deed of confidentiality, DPA, no-client-contact scope) to shorten diligence. Rush-deflation risk is hedged because the install pays back on throughput and backlog-driven comms load, which persist under either rule outcome (https://chambers.com/articles/ilr-processing-times-navigating-uk-settlement-delays).
- Net: failure case is real and MUST discount reach0 and ttfd, but does not invalidate the offer for the private-fee segment → defensible.
