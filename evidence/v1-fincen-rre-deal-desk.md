# FinCEN RRE Deal Desk
Category: Vertical SaaS | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Close all-cash entity/trust deals without FinCEN whiplash: a per-closing decision tree (covered transfer? exemption? renewed-GTO metro?), a secure beneficial-ownership data-collection portal that gets buyers to self-complete before the closing table, and filing-ready Real Estate Reports held in escrow — so the day the appeal reinstates the rule (or a GTO applies) you file in minutes, and if it stays vacated you've lost nothing but gained clean files for underwriter audits.
- Buyer: Owner/manager of an independent US title & escrow agency, 2–30 staff, handling residential closings with regular investor/LLC/trust cash purchases (FL, TX, AZ investor-heavy markets first).
- Price: $149/mo per agency; founding $79/mo for first 20 title agencies.
- Channel to first 10: Public member directories of state land title associations (FLTA, TLTA) for personal founder cold outreach; National Settlement Services Summit (NS3) / October Research LinkedIn community; title-agent Facebook groups. No founder email lists needed.
- Timing hypothesis: FinCEN's Residential Real Estate Rule took effect 1 March 2026 (nationwide, no price threshold, all non-financed transfers to entities/trusts reportable by settlement agents) — then a federal district court vacated it 19 March 2026; DOJ/FinCEN appeal is pending and FinCEN simultaneously renewed Geographic Targeting Orders. Title agencies spent Q1 building processes, tore them down in Q2, and now face a live dual-regime + snap-back risk with zero SMB tooling.
