# MTD Landlord Autopilot
Category: Vertical SaaS | Status: R1-generated 2026-07-10

## R1 Spec
- Offer: Every MTD for Income Tax quarterly update filed on time or that quarter's fee refunded. Bank-feed + rent-statement ingestion builds compliant digital records automatically (incl. jointly-owned property income splits — the known MTD nightmare case); the 7 Aug 2026 first submission is handled end-to-end. v1 = paid onboarding + records builder + deadline concierge exporting to recognised bridging software; direct HMRC API filing once vendor recognition granted.
- Buyer: Self-managing UK residential landlords, 3–15 properties, gross property (+ any sole-trade) income >£50,000, currently on spreadsheets + annual Self Assessment, no or minimal accountant.
- Price: £29/mo per landlord; founding-member £15/mo locked for first 100.
- Channel to first 10: Founding-member threads on PropertyTribes, Property118 and LandlordZone forums (free to post, exact ICP discussing MTD panic now); 692K cold list — UK landlord/property segment; NRLA-adjacent Facebook landlord groups.
- Timing hypothesis: MTD for Income Tax became mandatory 6 April 2026 for gross qualifying income >£50k; the FIRST quarterly update (6 Apr–5 Jul 2026 period) is due 7 August 2026 — four weeks from today. £200 penalty regime after points accrue; 12-month soft-landing on quarterly-update points only. April 2027 wave (> £30k) triples the market.
