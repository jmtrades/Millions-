## B. Notes

### B1. EU wave structure (the product's backbone)
Four distinct revenue moments remain after the Aug 2026 go-live: **2 Dec 2026** (machine-readable marking + PWD algorithmic management — a same-day double), **2 Aug 2027** (legacy GPAI backfill — forces retroactive model inventory), **2 Dec 2027** (Annex III full stack — the single largest module bundle: classification, FRIA, logging, EU-database registration, incident reporting), **2 Aug 2028** (embedded/Annex I — manufacturing verticals). Member-state implementing acts and authority designations continue rolling through 2026–27, creating per-country enforcement-contact and penalty-schedule content — a natural data-subscription layer. Each MS must also have a regulatory sandbox by 2 Aug 2026 (sandbox-application support = services attach).

### B2. US states — pattern recognition
The 2026–27 state wave has consolidated into four repeatable module archetypes: (1) **employment AEDT** (NYC, IL, CT; NJ AG has read NJLAD onto algorithmic discrimination via Jan 2025 guidance — no new date, existing-law exposure); (2) **provenance/detection** (CA SB 942 → UT HB 286 → WA 1170 — near-identical "covered provider >1M users" drafting, so one MRK module covers three states); (3) **frontier safety** (CA SB 53 → NY RAISE → CT CART — shared 10^26 FLOPs / $500M-revenue thresholds, shared INC module); (4) **companion chatbots** (NY GBL 47, CA SB 243, CT, WA — DIS+INC). Colorado (1 Jan 2027) remains the only full Colorado-style high-risk regime in force; Virginia's successor bills were mostly tabled to 2027 after the 2025 HB 2094 veto — watchlist, not a row.
**Preemption risk:** the Dec 2025 White House EO seeks to curb state AI laws (DOJ task force, funding leverage); NY enacted RAISE anyway. Treat as a discount factor on US-state row probability, not a reason to drop them — none of the dated rows above has been enjoined as of 11 Jul 2026.

### B3. Other majors
- **South Korea** is the largest non-EU catalog addition: full transparency/labeling/high-impact regime live 22 Jan 2026, with the 2026 grace period ending ~Jan 2027 — sell compliance-readiness during grace, enforcement modules after.
- **China + India** make marking-verification a global (not EU/CA) module: China labeling live 1 Sep 2025; India SGI labeling + 3-hour takedown live 20 Feb 2026.
- **Brazil PL 2338**: Chamber plenary vote was scheduled 27 May 2026 (rapporteur report 19 May); outcome not confirmed in this pass — if passed it returns to the Senate, then staggered vacatio (~180 days bans / ~2 years core duties) ⇒ obligations land 2027–28. Watchlist-high.
- **Canada**: AIDA dead (Jan 2025 prorogation); Feb 2026 national-AI-strategy consultation summary signals privacy-law route. No dated obligation.
- **UK**: no AI bill in the May 2026 King's Speech (a "Regulating for Growth Bill" was announced instead); regime = DUAA + ICO + sector regulators. FCA AI Live Testing cohort 2: applications Jan 2026, testing from Apr 2026, evaluation report Q1 2027 — supervisory expectations, a services/template attach, not a statutory module.
- **Japan**: promotion-only, no penalties — exclude from sellable rows.

### B4. Sector overlays
- **Financial services**: no new dated AI rules from EBA/FCA yet — the dated obligation for banks/insurers is the AI Act itself (creditworthiness + life/health pricing are Annex III ⇒ 2 Dec 2027 deployer duties). Package as an FS vertical template on the same engine.
- **Insurance (US)**: NAIC bulletin adoption (~24+ states) + Colorado Reg 10-1-1 make insurer AI inventories an examinable expectation now; Colorado DOI's follow-on quantitative-testing requirements for life underwriting (adopted 2025) add BIA — verify current phase before commit.
- **Employment/gig**: EU Platform Work Directive (2 Dec 2026) is the sleeper: algorithmic-management transparency for every digital labour platform in 27 national flavors.
- **Ads/content**: DSA (live) + TTPA (Oct 2025) + Art 50 (Aug 2026) chain ad-disclosure and synthetic-media marking into one DIS+MRK pipeline.
