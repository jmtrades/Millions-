## The structural truth
The Phase 0 filter (first £ <14 days, £0 spend, 8 build-hours, 7-day kills) selects AGAINST venture scale by construction. Barrier-to-start and ceiling are correlated: everything instantly monetizable by one person is contested precisely because it's cheap to start (81-candidate evidence base, 62 kills, dominant kill cause = "already served/free"). Venture-scale outcomes require a compounding loop (network effects, data moat, per-seat expansion, distribution lock) that takes quarters to ignite — they all fail a 14-day cash gate. Conclusion: do not replace the portfolio with a "billion-dollar idea" search under the same constraints; run two tracks with different physics.
